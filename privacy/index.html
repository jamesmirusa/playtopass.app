Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

GreenGo Partners LLC ("we") runs Play to Pass. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Anonymous profile. A random user ID created on your device, your study progress, settings and the nickname you choose for the league.
  • Email (optional). If you add it to sync progress or restore a subscription.
  • Subscription status. From Stripe: plan, status, renewal dates, your Stripe customer ID and the email used at checkout. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us.
  • Question reports you choose to send.

How we use it

To run the app, save and sync your progress, unlock Pro for subscribers, show the weekly league, answer support requests and improve questions. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

Supabase (database and sign-in), Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting) and Google Fonts. They process data on our behalf under their own terms.

Storage and retention

Progress is kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by writing to support; payment records are kept as required by law.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data and withdraw consent by contacting us. If you live in California, the EU/EEA or the UK you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honour them.

Children

The app is for medical students and graduates and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new effective date.
