Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

GreenGo Partners LLC ("we") runs Play to Pass. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Anonymous profile. A random user ID created on your device, your study progress, settings and the nickname you choose for the league.
  • Email (optional). If you add it to sync progress or restore a subscription.
  • Subscription status. From Stripe: plan, status, renewal dates, your Stripe customer ID and the email used at checkout. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us.
  • Question reports you choose to send.
  • Your answers. Which questions you answered, whether the answer was right, how long it took and your confidence, linked to your anonymous profile ID, so we can show your statistics and find questions that need fixing.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. Pages you open on playtopass.app, the website that sent you there (only its domain), campaign tags in the link (utm), device type, language, country and key app actions such as starting a test, opening the Pro screen or starting checkout. A random visitor ID is kept in your browser's local storage. These statistics of our own do not store your IP address. To opt out of them, open any page with ?notrack=1 at the end of the address; they also honour Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.
  • Product analytics by HeyCatch. We use HeyCatch (heycatch.ai) to understand how the site and app are used. It records the pages you open, clicks and screen changes, device and browser type, the referring site and campaign tags, and your approximate location (such as country), which it derives from your IP address. It keeps a random identifier in your browser (a cookie or local storage). In the app these events are linked to your anonymous user ID, and to your email and plan if you added an email or subscribed, together with events such as finishing onboarding and starting, converting or cancelling a subscription. The ?notrack=1, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings above apply to our own statistics only; to stop HeyCatch analytics, block heycatch.ai with a content blocker; to have data already collected deleted, write to support. We use no advertising cookies.

How we use it

To run the app, save and sync your progress, unlock Pro for subscribers, show the weekly league, answer support requests and improve questions. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

Supabase (database, sign-in and usage statistics), HeyCatch (product analytics), Stripe (payments), GitHub Pages (hosting), Resend (email) and Google Fonts. They process data on our behalf under their own terms.

Storage and retention

Progress is kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by writing to support; payment records are kept as required by law.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data and withdraw consent by contacting us. If you live in California, the EU/EEA or the UK you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honour them.

Children

The app is for medical students and graduates and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new effective date.
