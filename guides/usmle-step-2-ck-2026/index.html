USMLE Step 2 CK in 2026: 218 to pass, 16 blocks and how to prepare

Updated: 2026-11-19

Step 2 Clinical Knowledge asks what you would do next for a real patient. Since Step 1 became pass/fail, Step 2 CK is the main USMLE score residency programs compare — so it deserves a plan, not a cram.

The exam at a glance

ItemStep 2 CK in 2026
WherePrometric test centers, one long testing day
BlocksFrom May 7, 2026: 16 blocks of 30 minutes with about 18–20 questions each (previously 8 blocks of 60 minutes)
ScoreThree-digit score; the minimum passing score is 218 for exams taken on or after July 1, 2025 (previously 214)
Fee$695 USMLE program fee for a 2026 eligibility period, plus an international test delivery surcharge if you test outside the United States and Canada

Check fees, eligibility periods and scheduling on the official USMLE and ECFMG pages before you register.

What Step 2 CK tests

Step 1 asks why; Step 2 CK asks what now. Expect vignettes about diagnosis, the next best step in management, the most appropriate test, prevention and screening, and patient safety and ethics. Content spans internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, psychiatry, neurology and emergency care. Current guidelines matter: the right answer is what a careful physician would do today.

What score should you aim for?

218 is the line to pass, not a target. Programs use Step 2 CK to compare applicants, and competitive specialties expect scores well above the passing standard. Decide on your target specialty early, look up the scores of recently matched applicants, and plan to clear that range with a margin.

An 8–10 week dedicated plan

  1. Week 1 — baseline. One timed self-assessment. List your weakest disciplines and systems.
  2. Weeks 2–6 — discipline blocks. Rotate medicine, surgery, pediatrics, OB/GYN and psychiatry. Daily: 60–80 timed questions in 30-minute blocks, then a careful review of every explanation. Add the rules you missed to your spaced-repetition deck.
  3. Weeks 7–8 — random and timed. Mixed blocks only, full days twice a week to build stamina for sixteen blocks.
  4. Final week. One last self-assessment early in the week, then targeted review of your error log. Rest the day before.

Habits that raise Step 2 CK scores

  • Think in algorithms. For each presentation, know the first test, the confirmatory test and the treatment — and when the patient is too unstable for any of that.
  • Read the last line first. Know whether you are being asked for a diagnosis, a test or a treatment before you read the vignette.
  • Watch the vital signs. Instability changes the answer: stabilize first, investigate later.
  • Review your right answers too. A lucky guess is a future miss.
