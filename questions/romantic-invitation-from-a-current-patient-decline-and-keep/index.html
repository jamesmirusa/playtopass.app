USMLE Step 2 CK practice question: Romantic invitation from a current patient: decline and keep the relationship professional

USMLE Step 2 CKEthicsEthics

A 34-year-old man has seen his 36-year-old female primary care physician for 2 years for management of asthma. At the end of a routine visit he says he has enjoyed getting to know her and asks whether she would like to have dinner with him this weekend. Both are single. The physician has no romantic interest in him and wants to continue as his doctor.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response by the physician?

  1. Accept, because both are consenting adults
  2. Transfer his care to a colleague so they can date
  3. Politely decline and keep the relationship professional
  4. End the patient-physician relationship immediately
  5. Report the request to the state medical board
Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Politely decline and keep the relationship professional

A romantic or sexual relationship with a current patient is unethical, because the physician's power, the patient's trust and access to private information make true consent impossible and can harm care. The appropriate response is to decline kindly and clearly and to keep the relationship professional; the physician may continue caring for the patient as long as the boundary holds. Transferring care in order to start a relationship does not make it acceptable, and relationships with former patients are also discouraged when the physician uses trust or knowledge gained in the clinical relationship. Ending care is unnecessary unless the patient cannot respect the boundary, and a respectful invitation is not misconduct to report.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. Accept, because both are consenting adults — Consent is not considered free in a relationship with a current patient, so dating is unethical.
  • B. Transfer his care to a colleague so they can date — Transferring care to begin a relationship exploits trust built in the clinical relationship.
  • D. End the patient-physician relationship immediately — Ending care is unnecessary when the patient can accept the boundary.
  • E. Report the request to the state medical board — A respectful invitation is not misconduct that requires reporting.

Key point

Respond to a romantic invitation from a patient by declining and maintaining professional boundaries

Memory hook

Patient asks you out: say no kindly, keep caring for him—never date a current patient.
