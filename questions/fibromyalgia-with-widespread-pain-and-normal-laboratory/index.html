USMLE Step 2 CK practice question: Fibromyalgia with widespread pain and normal laboratory tests: aerobic exercise and education as first-line treatment

USMLE Step 2 CKMusculoskeletalInternal Medicine

A 42-year-old woman comes to the physician because of widespread pain for 1 year affecting both sides of her body above and below the waist. She also has fatigue, unrefreshing sleep and difficulty concentrating. Over-the-counter analgesics have not helped. Her vital signs are normal. There is diffuse tenderness to palpation of the neck, shoulders, back and thighs without joint swelling or weakness. Complete blood count, erythrocyte sedimentation rate, C-reactive protein, thyroid-stimulating hormone and creatine kinase are within the reference ranges.

Which of the following is the most appropriate initial management?

  1. Graded aerobic exercise and education
  2. Oral oxycodone
  3. Oral prednisone
  4. Daily oral ibuprofen long term
  5. MRI of the entire spine
Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Graded aerobic exercise and education

Chronic widespread pain with fatigue, nonrestorative sleep and cognitive symptoms, normal inflammatory markers, muscle enzymes and thyroid function, and no synovitis or weakness fit fibromyalgia, a disorder of central pain processing (nociplastic pain). Management begins with education that the pain is real but does not reflect tissue damage, followed by nonpharmacologic therapy; in the revised European Alliance of Associations for Rheumatology recommendations, exercise, especially graded aerobic and strengthening exercise, is the only intervention with a strong recommendation. Cognitive behavioral therapy, sleep hygiene, mind-body therapies and, for persistent severe symptoms, low-dose amitriptyline, duloxetine or pregabalin can be added. Opioids are ineffective in fibromyalgia, worsen hyperalgesia and carry addiction risk. Corticosteroids have no benefit because there is no inflammation. Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs are not effective for the core pain and cause gastrointestinal and renal toxicity with long-term use. Imaging is not indicated in the absence of red flags and can lead to incidental findings and unnecessary procedures.

Why the other options are wrong

  • B. Oral oxycodone — Opioids are ineffective in fibromyalgia and can worsen pain sensitivity and cause dependence.
  • C. Oral prednisone — There is no inflammation, and corticosteroids do not help fibromyalgia.
  • D. Daily oral ibuprofen long term — NSAIDs do not treat central pain sensitization and cause toxicity with long-term use.
  • E. MRI of the entire spine — Imaging without red flags finds incidental changes and does not guide treatment.

Key point

Begin fibromyalgia treatment with education and graded aerobic exercise, adding CBT or low-dose amitriptyline, duloxetine or pregabalin if needed.

Memory hook

Fibromyalgia: move more, explain more; opioids make it worse.
